# Energy analog

We have been usiong food/resource as an analog for energy; food enables reproduction, existence is taxed, food fed to the environmnet keeps agents going via their eating.  

But there is something bothersome in the analogy between food and energy.  Life actually needs an *energy gradient*,  not simply energy.  In a deep ocean vent, there is a strong temperature gradieng between the vent and the surrounding ocean.  A human running a race will actually be much more effective at an ambient temperature less than body temperature.  Cell metabolisms have internal energy gradients with their ATP chemistry.

Where is the idea of energy gradient in EvoCA.  Is it missing?  Is there a way to put it in to make the model more realistic and ultimately more evolvable?
