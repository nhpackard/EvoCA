# References for EvoCA research-directions analysis

Files downloaded into this directory where licensing/access allowed.
Paywalled items list the publisher landing page (the "paywall link"
per the request). Nothing here is a fabricated citation; items I could
not verify a stable identifier for are omitted rather than guessed.

## Foundational activity-statistics series (Packard et al.)

- **Bedau, M. A., Packard, N. H. (1992).** *Measurement of
  evolutionary activity, teleology, and life.* In Artificial Life II
  (Langton, Taylor, Farmer, Rasmussen, eds.), Addison-Wesley,
  pp. 431–461.
- **Bedau, M. A., Snyder, E., Brown, C. T., Packard, N. H. (1997).**
  *A comparison of evolutionary activity in artificial evolving
  systems and in the biosphere.* In Proc. 4th European Conf. on
  Artificial Life (ECAL 97), MIT Press, pp. 125–134.
- **Bedau, M. A., Snyder, E., Packard, N. H. (1998).** *A

## Landscapes, criticality, mutation, thermodynamics (paywall links)
