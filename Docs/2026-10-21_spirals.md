## Findings (example runs, not campaign results)

- **One recipe makes spirals:** `Research/Resource-driving/Runs/2026-10-15_R1cfg208_intermediate_fi0p016.evoca`, final metaparams (`m_scale` 3.5, `food_inc` 0.016, `tax` 0.035, `gdiff` 0.08, `food_cap=0` recorded). Clean at N=512, cramped into arcs at N=256. The free end of an arm winds around its core in a 20-step film.
- **"Resource-driving" is the question, not a mechanism.** The recipe uses uniform regrowth like every RD recipe (`food_per` unset). It is R1 scan row 208 (the Resource-driving workstream's `m_scale`/`food_inc` bracket) kept at an intermediate `food_inc`. What differs is the regime: `m_scale` 3.5, while every RD scan stopped at 1.2 (RD recipes reach 1.5).
- **A narrow window.** Spirals at (`m_scale` 2.5, `food_inc` 0.013) and (3.5, 0.016); crescent fronts at (3.5, 0.013); none at `m_scale` 1.2 or 5.0 or at `food_inc` >= 0.020; extinct at (3.5, 0.010). They sit just above the extinction edge (alive 0.24-0.28). Faster eating alone does not make them (`m_scale` 5.0 has none), contrary to the first guess.
- **Diffusion window.** `gdiff` 0 and 0.02 go extinct, 0.08 gives spirals, 0.3 and 1.0 give a uniform texture.
- **Seeds.** All three seeds show spirals; only seed 0 fills the lattice.
- **They need the legacy uncapped mouthful.** With `food_cap=1` the recipe is a uniform texture, and the whole 5 x 5 grid under `food_cap=1` has no spirals. Under `food_cap=0`, 7.2% of the lattice has `F` < 0 (min -0.61) at t=1600; none under `food_cap=1`. Working hypothesis (not demonstrated): the overdraw leaves a food deficit behind each front that acts as the refractory zone.
- **Consequence.** Since the A9 fix removed the overdraw as unphysical, these spirals are a property of the legacy rule at this parameter point; whether the corrected model makes spirals is open (RXD-N6).
- **Target waves.** `foodinc013_osc` (Criticality, `m_scale` 3.5) gives target waves (rings from point sources), not spirals, at N=256 and N=512.

## Update 2026-10-23 (N)

The standard model is the capped rule (`food_cap=1`), and these spirals are a legacy-rule result. N's conclusion: the model has no mechanism that creates a refractory period, so it makes no spiral (scroll) waves; this is a property of the model class. Refractory gates on eating did not change this (`Docs/2026-10-22_food-thresh.md`). Tweaks that force a refractory period without modeling justification are ruled out. The acceptable path is an inhibitor/waste field, the chem model's milestone 2. The notebook material on these spirals is now in the Reaction-diffusion notebook's final section, "Legacy rule (`food_cap=0`)"; see `Docs/2026-10-23_food-cap-standard.md`.
